# LƯỜI ENGLISH — World Design & Environmental Storytelling

> **World Systems**: 2 Living Environments (Cozy Treehouse & Explorer Island)  
> **Architecture**: Layered Parallax Environment Rendering

---

## 1. Multi-Layer Environmental Architecture

Every world background is constructed with 6 distinct rendering layers:

```
[Layer 0] Background Sky & Atmospheric Gradients (Slow drifting clouds / sunbeams)
   ↓
[Layer 1] Distant Landmarks (Mountains, distant islands, giant canopy branches)
   ↓
[Layer 2] Midground Environment (Treehouse library, rope bridges, waterfalls, camps)
   ↓
[Layer 3] Interactive Learning Path (Winding S-curve trail with learning nodes)
   ↓
[Layer 4] Foreground Nature & Decor (Flower bushes, lanterns, floating leaves, fireflies)
   ↓
[Layer 5] Mascot & Character Actor Layer (Chú Lười positioning & dialogue bubble)
   ↓
[Layer 6] Particle FX & Celebration Overlay (Sparkles, star bursts, heart cascades)
```

## 2. World A: Cozy Treehouse ("Ngôi Nhà Cây Kì Diệu")

- **Lore**: The ancient Banyan Tree of Wisdom where Chú Lười lives and gathers all the world's English storybooks.
- **Key Visual Elements**:
  - Warm wooden treehouse with round glowing glass windows.
  - Floating paper lanterns giving a gentle golden glow.
  - Wooden bookshelves nestled in hollow tree trunks.
  - Little songbirds perching on leafy branches.
  - Drifting fireflies at twilight.
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Honey Amber (`#F59E0B`), Sage Emerald (`#10B981`), Warm Cream (`#FFFBEB`), Dusty Rose (`#FB7185`).

---

## 3. World B: Explorer Island ("Đảo Phiêu Lưu Kì Thú")

- **Lore**: An uncharted tropical archipelago with ancient English stone tablets, mystery caves, and hidden treasure chests.
- **Key Visual Elements**:
  - Golden sandy beaches and turquoise ocean waves.
  - Rope suspension bridges connecting island peaks.
  - Lush jungle vines with hidden banana clusters.
  - Explorer camp with tent, campfire, and telescope.
  - Ancient stone ruins carved with English letters.
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Tropical Sky (`#0EA5E9`), Sunset Gold (`#F59E0B`), Jungle Leaf (`#10B981`), Coral Red (`#F43F5E`).
