# LƯỜI ENGLISH — Character Bible: Chú Lười (The Sloth)

> **PRIMARY MASCOT**: **CHÚ LƯỜI** (The Friendly Sloth)  
> **BRAND MOTTO**: *"Lười học mà vẫn giỏi."* (Effortless learning through smart retention)  
> **STRICT IP RULE**: **Dino / Dinosaur is strictly forbidden as our mascot.** DinoEnglish may only be cited as competitor research. Never create dinosaur mascots, dino assets, or dino component names.

---

## 1. Character Identity & Personality

Chú Lười is an endearing, warm, and highly relatable sloth who represents the dream of every child: learning English effortlessly, naturally, and with plenty of fun. He is never lazy in a negative way—rather, he is calm, observant, deeply encouraging, and finds the smartest, most enjoyable ways to master English.

### Core Character Traits:
- **Gentle & Patient**: Speaks calmly, never judges mistakes, celebrates small wins.
- **Clever & Inquisitive**: Finds clever memory tricks and fun analogies.
- **Playful Companion**: Loves eating snacks, exploring new worlds, and reading stories.

---

## 2. Anatomy & Visual Consistency

Chú Lười has a recognizable, standardized anatomy across all illustrations and themes:
- **Body**: Soft, rounded shape with warm honey-caramel / golden-tan fur and a cream-colored facial mask.
- **Eyes**: Large, expressive, warm dark-brown eyes with friendly eye-patch markings typical of a three-toed sloth.
- **Claws/Hands**: Three rounded, soft, mitten-like claws (stylized for children, non-threatening).
- **Mouth**: Small curved smile, highly expressive for speaking and laughing.

## 4. Standard Pose & Expression Matrix

### Core Poses (Asset ID prefix: `mascot.sloth.<theme>.<pose>`):
1. `idle` — Relaxed standing or sitting, gentle breathing.
2. `hello` — Waving a friendly paw at the child.
3. `happy` — Small hop with paws raised in joy.
4. `thinking` — Chin resting on paw, looking up thoughtfully.
5. `listening` — Paw to ear, leaning forward attentively.
6. `speaking` — Open mouth, gesturing smoothly as if narrating.
7. `reading` — Holding a storybook with focus.
8. `writing` — Holding a giant pencil or stylus.
9. `playing` — Playing with letter blocks or a ball.
10. `running` — Slow-motion funny "fast" sloth run.
11. `celebrating` — Throwing confetti with huge smile.
12. `sleeping` — Curling up with a small "Zzz" bubble.
13. `eating` — Munching on pet snacks or leaves.
14. `pointing` — Pointing towards the learning question / card.
15. `confused` — Scratching head with a question mark above.
16. `encourage` — Thumbs up with both paws, warm smile.

### Core Expressions:
`smile`, `laugh`, `excited`, `surprised`, `confused`, `sleepy`, `proud`, `love`.
