# LƯỜI ENGLISH — Product Context

## Vision
To build the most engaging, scientifically sound English acquisition platform for Vietnamese primary and secondary students (Grade 1–12), where students learn effortlessly through smart spaced repetition, active recall, and joyful storytelling.

## Brand Mascot
**Chú Lười** — A friendly, wise, and adorable sloth wearing cozy knitwear (Cozy theme) or explorer gear (Explorer theme). Dino/dinosaur mascot is strictly forbidden.

## Product Worlds
1. **Home / Dashboard**: Daily goals, streak, active pet companion, daily recommendation.
2. **Adventure Map**: Visual journey through themed islands matching grade level topics.
3. **School**: Direct alignment with standard Vietnamese school textbooks (e.g. Global Success units).
4. **Skills Hub**: Focused practice for Vocabulary, Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Grammar, Phonics, Daily Sentences.
5. **Talk to Lười**: AI conversational practice (Free Talk, Role Play, Daily Interview, Story Retell).
6. **Story World**: Read-along animated stories with tap-to-listen vocabulary.
7. **Media World**: Curated short video immersion, kids songs, and news.
8. **Game Land**: Pedagogical mini-games reinforcing chunks and sentence structures.
9. **Pet World**: Incubating, feeding, and nurturing Chú Lười pet companion.
10. **Progress & Parent Portal**: True competency metrics (vocabulary recall, active speaking minutes).
