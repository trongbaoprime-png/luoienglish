# LƯỜI ENGLISH — Child Safety & Privacy Checklist (COPPA / GDPR-K)

> **Mandatory Gate**: Use for any feature involving children profiles, learning experiences, or gamification.

---

## 1. Zero PII (Personally Identifiable Information)
- [ ] No real full names, home addresses, phone numbers, or exact geo-locations stored for children.
- [ ] Children identified only by `nickname` (e.g. "Bé Bơ") and sloth avatar selection.
- [ ] No public social feeds, direct messaging between children, or open profile search.

## 2. Protected Child Experience
- [ ] Child Mode (`/home`, `/learn/**`, `/adventure-map`, `/pet`) contains zero advertisements or promotional banners.
- [ ] Zero un-gated external web links or external social login prompts in Child Mode.
- [ ] In-app purchases, billing, account deletion, and email changes are strictly locked behind the Parental Gate.

## 3. Brand & Pedagogical Tone
- [ ] Mascot is strictly **CHÚ LƯỜI** (a clever, cozy SLOTH).
- [ ] Feedback is encouraging, stress-free, and supportive ("Lười học mà vẫn giỏi").
- [ ] No punitive timers, shaming text, or stressful streak-loss penalties.
