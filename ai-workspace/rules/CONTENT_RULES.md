# LƯỜI ENGLISH — Content Rules

1. **Original Intellectual Property**:
   - All sentences, stories, questions, and dialogues must be original creations.
   - Reference standard school topics for alignment, but never copy copyrighted textbook text verbatim.
2. **Data-Driven Architecture**:
   - Content must be authored as structured JSON/TypeScript models conforming to `Curriculum`, `Unit`, `Lesson`, `KnowledgeItem`, and `Activity` schemas.
   - Never embed lesson content directly into React components.
3. **Age Appropriateness & Encouraging Tone**:
   - Content for Grade 1–5 must be cheerful, positive, clear, and phonetically suitable for young Vietnamese learners.
