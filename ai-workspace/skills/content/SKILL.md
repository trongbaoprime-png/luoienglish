## Rules:
- Author lessons as data fixtures under `src/content/`.
- Every lesson must follow: Word -> Chunk -> Sentence -> Question -> Answer -> Conversation -> Situation.
- Map knowledge items to acquisition stages (recognize, recall, understand, use, produce, transfer).
- Ensure 100% original IP, completely free from copyright infringement.
